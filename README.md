## 📊 Projects & Research

### 💼 Performance-Based Pay & Worker Output — Prolific Field Experiment
**ResEcon 428 · Team:** Abha Dewal, Anjalika Arora, Akanksha Sharma

- Designed and ran a natural field experiment on Prolific.com (n = 44) comparing fixed pay ($2.00) with bonus-eligible pay ($1.50 + $0.50 quality bonus)
- Bonus group wrote **~103 more words** on average (p < 0.001, R² = 0.68); effect held at ~98 words after demographic controls
- Quality scores rose **3.01 points** (p = 0.0002) and typo rate fell **0.034 per 10 words** (p = 0.0076)
- Takeaway: clear, performance-linked criteria outperformed a higher guaranteed payment

`OLS Regression` `T-tests` `Qualtrics` `Prolific` `Excel` `Experimental Design`

### 🍺 Alcohol Consumption & Life Expectancy in South & Southeast Asia
**Econometrics · Individual project**

- Analyzed WHO panel data for South and Southeast Asian countries (2000–2015) in RStudio
- Modeled life expectancy on log GDP, alcohol consumption, region, and interaction terms
- GDP was the strongest predictor, with a larger effect in Southeast Asia; alcohol showed no significant effect in either low (<1.0L, p = 0.94) or high (>1.0L, p = 0.069) consumption groups
- Presented as a research poster with ggplot2 visualizations

`R Studio` `ggplot2` `OLS Regression` `Interaction Terms` `WHO Data`
